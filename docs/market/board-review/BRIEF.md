# Shared brief for the Cgate C-suite

## The company
Cgate (also called Yardway in older docs) is UK equine management software. Django app in /home/user/CGate/horse_management. Built for one UK grazing and stud business (7 sites). Today it is single-tenant (no organisations, no self-serve sign-up, no subscription billing, no Stripe, no WhatsApp).

Built today: horses, owners, fractional ownership, placements and daily rates, monthly invoicing with PDF/email and Xero sync, health (vaccinations, farrier, worming, egg counts, vet visits), breeding (coverings, scans, foaling), field maps and boundaries, rest and rotation, barns, stables and turnout, roles and permissions, mobile PWA, a farrier visit page (phone), and C-vet: an offline vet visit app (notes, voice, price list, bill to the yard) that sits beside a practice's own software. One vet (Tim) is trialling C-vet; one farrier test ran.

## The founder
Has strong personal contacts in the UK equine and racing worlds that can unlock racing trainers, livery yards, studs, vets and farriers. Treat this as a real asset: warm introductions, faster first sales, lower cost to win customers, possible design partners and early adopters. The founder wants to run an AI-first company: a very small human team, with AI agents doing much of the coding, testing, support, onboarding, data import, sales admin and content.

## The founder's decisions so far
- Modular pricing: a cheap core (£2.50 per horse per month) plus paid add-on modules (fields £1, stables £1, breeding £1.50, race training £2, owner updates £2).
- Race training-fee payments removed (Weatherbys already collects them). Payment links on livery invoices are on the roadmap (3 weeks of work).
- Long-term wish: become the vet practice's main software, not a side app.
- Early race trainers can get a hosted separate install with a set-up fee before multi-tenant is ready.
- Long-term vision: a horse identity and life timeline (traceability ledger) and later marketplaces (feed, transport).
