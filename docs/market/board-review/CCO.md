# CCO proposal: go-to-market and pricing

## Summary
Sell first to what is already built and what the founder's contacts open: studs, grazing and pre-training yards, then race trainers, then vets on C-vet. Do not use the founder to sell to livery yards. Livery yards are small, price-sensitive and close often (the model assumes 30% are lost each year), so they should come later through LiveryList and self-serve sign-up. Every early deal is paid: a 30-day paid start (£250, taken off the first year's bill) that becomes a 12-month contract unless the customer cancels. Keep the £2.50 core price. Put the modules into three simple plans with minimum monthly fees. Make the farrier app free. Charge race yards a £1,000 set-up fee, and let syndicates pay for owner updates. The race bundle sells features that are not built yet: training records, owner updates and WhatsApp do not exist in the code. So the first race trainers must sign as paying design partners with an agreed build plan. My model test changes stud timing, the farrier price, set-up fees and the cost to win a customer. It raises 2031 ARR from £1.14m to £1.18m and cuts the amount to raise from £1.96m to £1.71m. With the vet build also dropped, the raise falls to £1.12m. Investor return stays about 1x. Better selling alone does not make Cgate investable. It needs more horses per customer, more revenue per horse and low churn.

| Type | Number | Why |
|---|---|---|

| Pre-training, breaking and rehab yards | 3 | Use fields, health and invoicing. They link studs to trainers. |
| Race trainers, 20 to 60 horses | 5 | Hosted install. Paying design partners for the training and owner modules. |
| Large livery or competition yards, 30+ horses, complex invoicing | 3 | Xero sync and invoicing are a real pain. Hosted install. |
| Equine vet practices on C-vet | 2 | Tim plus one. Bills go to yards, so each practice brings in yards. |
| Racing syndicate manager | 1 | Pays for owner updates directly. |

**Design partners (5):** 2 trainers, 2 studs, 1 vet. Each pays the founding price, joins a monthly feedback call, agrees to a case study and gives two introductions. In return they get the price fixed for 24 months at 20% off, and a say in what we build.

**Referral loops** (reward: one free month for both sides when a referral pays):
- **Vet to yard:** C-vet bills the yard. A yard not on Cgate gets the visit notes by email with an invitation to join.
- **Farrier to yard:** the farrier app is free. Each visit record is sent to the yard and carries an invitation.
- **Stud to pre-trainer to trainer:** the horse's record (foaling, vaccinations, worming) moves with the horse. This is the start of the life timeline.
- **Trainer to owners:** owners see updates. Owners who also have mares at studs pull those studs in.

**Turning contacts into contracts, not free pilots:**
- Write a one-page success plan before the start, with three measurable goals (for example, invoices out by day 3).
- Charge for the start. It turns into an annual contract automatically.
- Charge for the data import, with the work done by AI.
- Have no "free until ready" deals.

## 3. Pricing

**Competitor prices:**
- Yardman: from about $75 a month (https://sourceforge.net/software/product/Yardman-Stud/).
- miStable: $40 a month up to 15 horses, $80 for 16 to 49, $110 unlimited, probably in AUD (https://mistable.com/pricing, https://mistable.com/sign-up).
- TLore Owner's View: $4 per horse per month, or $2 when the trainer pays under a 2025 offer (https://pastthewire.com/tjc-innovations-releases-tlore-owner-app/).
- Equestrian Systems: £50 to £135 a month (https://www.getapp.co.uk/software/2068406/equestrian-management-system).
- LIVERYLive: £2 per horse per month, usage-based (https://www.capterra.com/p/10047219/LIVERYLive/).

**How our prices compare:**
- **Yard only** is £2.70 per horse. That is in line with LIVERYLive, which costs a little less. Fair.
- **Yard + Field** is £102 a month. That is at the top of the Equestrian Systems range, and value is only 2.2x the price. Hard sale.
- **Race bundle** is about £300 a month for 40 horses. That is 3 to 4 times Yardman or miStable. It holds only if owner updates and training records are real and good.
- **All four bundles** show value under 3x the price, which the model itself calls a hard sale. The hours-saved figures are guesses and need testing in interviews.

**Recommended packaging:** keep the modules inside the product, but sell three plans.

| Plan | Price | Minimum | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yard (livery) | £2.50 per horse per month | £29 a month | Core. Fields +£1. |
| Stud and Farm | £6.50 per horse per month | £99 a month | Core, fields, stables, breeding |
| Race Yard | £5.50 per horse per month | £149 a month | Core, stables, training. Fields +£1. |
| Owner updates | £2 per horse per month (yard pays) or £3 per horse per month (syndicate or owner pays) | n/a | Email digest first, WhatsApp later |
| Farrier app | Free | n/a | Brings in yards |
| C-vet | £60 per vet per month | n/a | Side app only, until a practice pays for more |

- **Annual contracts:** paid yearly in advance with two months free, or monthly on a 12-month term. Livery can pay month to month.
- **Set-up fees:** £1,000 for a hosted race yard, £500 for a stud or large yard (includes data import), £0 for self-serve.
- **No free tier for yards.** Owners and farriers are free; they are how we grow.
- **Owner updates:** let the syndicate or owner pay where the trainer wants to recharge it, like sundries on the training bill. Do not depend on collecting money from four owners per horse.

## 4. Other revenue: an honest view
- **Set-up and data import:** real and needed now. £20k to £40k a year (estimate).
- **Premium owner report** (monthly PDF with photos, health and costs) at £5 per owner per month: worth testing with syndicates. Small.
- **Sale pack** for Tattersalls and Goffs (vet, worming and vaccination history), at about £25 per horse: promising for studs and consignors. Test it.
- **Payments:** the fee is capped at £1, so it adds little revenue. The real value is keeping customers.
- **Insurance referrals:** need FCA introducer rules followed. Small commissions. Later.
- **Feed partnerships:** affiliate fees of 3 to 5%. Too small before 500 yards.
- **Data services:** the data set is too small, and GDPR and owner consent are hard. Not before 2029.

## 6. AI agents and humans
**AI agents can do:**
- Data import from spreadsheets and Yardman exports.
- Onboarding checklists.
- First-line support by email and chat.
- Demo data.
- Drafting proposals and contracts.
- Keeping the CRM up to date and sending follow-ups.
- Interview notes and summaries.
- Warnings when a customer may leave, based on usage.
- SEO content.
- Drafting owner updates from yard notes and photos.

**Humans must do:**
- First meetings and relationships.
- Price negotiation and signing.
- Design partner calls.
- Partnership deals with NTF, TBA and LiveryList.
- Calls to save a customer who wants to cancel.
- Disputes over vet bills or clinical records.
- Horse welfare incidents.
- Final sign-off on data protection requests.
