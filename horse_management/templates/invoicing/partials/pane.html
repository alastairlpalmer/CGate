{% load ui_extras %} {% comment %} Invoice preview — the body of the invoice list's side pane, and of its sheet on a phone (invoicing.views.invoice_pane). Three views of one invoice, on a rail at the top of the pane: Invoice — what the invoice page says, sized for a pane Owner — the owner it bills: the same record the owner list's pane shows PDF — the file itself, as the owner gets it The rail is the `paneTabs` component in static/js/split_view.js. The tab is remembered for the page, so the arrow keys flick through the same view of each invoice. A row's owner link opens the pane on the Owner tab (the `split-tab` event below). The pane's own chrome (previous/next, the counter, Open full page, close) is in invoice_list.html and stays put across swaps. No ids on the tabs or the views. After a swap htmx puts back the attributes of every element whose id was also in the old content, which undid the class and the display that Alpine had just set: the rail showed one tab and the pane another. {% endcomment %}
