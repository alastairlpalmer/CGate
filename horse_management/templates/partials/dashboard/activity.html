What a viewer sees follows their role: activity.recent only reads the sources the role may view (horses, health, charges, invoices, feed), so an administrator sees every kind and a groom sees no money. The filter (the funnel in the header) lists the groups that are left; with one group there is no filter. Each row: a tinted glyph for the kind, the horses (or owner, or location) as chips with their coat colour, what happened, and any money on the right. A farrier round or a batch of vaccinations is one row naming every horse. On a wide screen the card is as tall as the calendar and map beside it and its list scrolls and shows every row. On a phone the first rows show and the rest fold behind "Show N earlier changes" (core.views.dashboard._fold_activity). A filter shows every row of its group. {% endcomment %}
