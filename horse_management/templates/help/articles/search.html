Use the search to find a horse, an owner, a location, an invoice or a health record from any page.

Open the search

  1. On a computer, select Search at the top of the sidebar.
  2. On a phone, select the search box at the top of the screen.

To open the search from the keyboard, press /. You can also press Ctrl+K, or ⌘K on a Mac.

Find a record

  1. Type two letters or more.
  2. Read the matches. They show in groups, for example Horses and Owners.
  3. Select a match to open its page.

The search finds:

  • Horses by name, microchip number or passport number.
  • Owners by name.
  • Locations by name or site.
  • Invoices by number.
  • Health records by condition or by the reason for a vet visit.

A spelling that is almost correct also finds a match.

Use the keyboard

  1. Press the down arrow to move to the first match.
  2. Press the up arrow or the down arrow to move between the matches.
  3. Press Enter to open the match.
  4. Press Esc to close the search.

Search with a narrow sidebar

  1. Select the magnifying glass at the top of the narrow sidebar.
  2. Type in the box that opens next to the sidebar.

Common problems

The search does not find an invoice. Why?
The search shows only the areas that your role can open. If you cannot open Invoices, the search does not show invoices.
The search shows nothing after one letter. Is this correct?
Yes. Type two letters or more to start the search.
