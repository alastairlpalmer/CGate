Cgate keeps the records of your yard in one place: horses, locations, health, feed, owners and money. This guide shows how to move around the app.

Use the sidebar

On a computer, the sidebar is on the left of each page. From top to bottom, it has:

  • Search, to find a horse, owner, location or invoice.
  • The areas of the app, in three groups: Overview, Manage and Finance. Under Finance, Finances holds four tabs: Overview, Invoices, Charges and Costs.
  • Notifications, Support and Settings.
  • Your profile card, with your name and email.

You see only the areas that your role can open. An administrator sets the roles.

Make the sidebar narrow

  1. Select the double arrow at the top of the sidebar, next to Cgate.
  2. To make the sidebar wide again, put the pointer on the logo at the top.
  3. Select the arrow that shows in place of the logo.

A narrow sidebar shows only the icons. Put the pointer on an icon to see its name. Cgate keeps your choice on this computer.

Use Cgate on a phone

  1. Use the bar at the bottom of the screen to open Dashboard, Horses or Locations.
  2. Select More to open the other areas, your profile, help and Sign Out.
  3. Select the bell at the top left to open your notifications.
  4. On the dashboard, the first list shows what is due today and in the next 7 days. Select Month to see the calendar.

You can put Cgate on your phone as an app: see Install Cgate on your phone.

Read the dashboard

The dashboard shows the state of the yard today.

  • The figures at the top show the yard at a glance: horses, owners, capacity, notifications, mares in foal and money owed. When your yard uses barns, capacity shows fields and stables apart. You see only the figures for the areas that your role can open. Select Notifications to open the list of things that need doing.
  • The calendar shows the current month. A dot on a day shows that something is due on that day. Select Visits to book together under the calendar to see visits that you can book as one.
  • To put your own item on the calendar (for example, a hay delivery), select a day, then select Add. To make it repeat (every day, week, 2 weeks, month or year), choose Repeat, and set Until if it has an end. To change or delete it, select the day, then select Edit on the item. For a repeating item you can delete one day only, or all days.
  • Near you shows a map of your site under the calendar, when your yard has set up its maps. A small tag on a rested field shows how many days it has rested.
  • The Timeline on the right shows what changed, newest first. Select the filter button (the funnel) to show only one type of item, for example Health. You see only the types of item that your role can open.
  • The Money section is at the bottom.

Use the calendar

  1. Select a day in the calendar.
  2. Read the list of what is due on that day, under the calendar.
  3. Select a button in the list to record the work, for example Record vaccination.
  4. Use the arrows at the top right of the calendar to see the previous month or the next month.
  5. Select Today to go back to the current month.

A day with a red outline has work that is overdue.

Add a record quickly

  1. Select the + button at the top right of any page.
  2. Select what to add, for example Log arrival, Add horse, Add location or Add owner.

Common problems

An area is not in my sidebar. Why?
Your role does not give you access to that area. Ask an administrator of your yard to change your role.
How do I hide a section of the dashboard?
Open Settings. In the Dashboard section, turn off the sections that you do not want.
The calendar is empty. Is this correct?
Yes, when nothing is due in that month. The calendar uses the next due dates on the records of your horses.
