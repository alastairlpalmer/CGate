Charges are the extra costs that you bill to owners. Invoices collect the livery and the unbilled charges for each owner, and record the payments.

Add an extra charge

  1. Select Charges in the sidebar.
  2. Select Add Charge.
  3. In Horses, tick one or more horses.
  4. Select the Type and enter the Description, the amount and the Date.
  5. Select Add Charge.

Each ticked horse gets its own charge for the full amount. Cgate bills the current owner of each horse, unless you select an owner in Bill To (Owner). A cost on a health or farrier record also makes a charge.

You need full access to Charges to add a charge.

Make the monthly invoices

On the 1st of each month, Cgate makes draft invoices for the month before. An administrator can turn this off in Settings, Business, Auto-generate monthly draft invoices.

  1. To make the invoices yourself, select Invoices in the sidebar.
  2. Select Generate Monthly.
  3. Select the Year and the Month.
  4. Select Generate Invoices.

Each invoice has the livery and all unbilled charges. Cgate skips an owner who already has an invoice for that month. To make one invoice, select Create Invoice.

Check and send an invoice

  1. On the Invoices page, select the invoice.
  2. Check the lines. To change them, select More, then Edit invoice.
  3. Select Send Invoice.

Cgate emails the invoice to the owner. To send many drafts, tick them on the Invoices page and select Send Selected. To remove a draft, select More, then Delete Draft.

Record a payment

  1. Open the invoice.
  2. Select Record Payment.
  3. Enter the date, the amount and the method.
  4. Select Record Payment.

Part payments are fine. The invoice stays open until the balance is zero. If the owner paid the full amount, you can select More, then Mark as Paid. To mark many invoices, tick them and select Mark Selected Paid.

To lower what an owner owes on a sent invoice, select More, then Issue Credit Note. You need full access to Invoices for these tasks.

Follow up overdue invoices

When the due date of a sent invoice is in the past, its status changes to Overdue. Cgate then emails reminders to the owner at set intervals. The last reminder is a final notice. After it, Cgate sends no more reminders.

The invoice page shows Reminders Sent. To stop the reminders for one owner, edit the owner and select Pause overdue reminders.

Paid and overdue invoices also show in the Money category of your notifications.

  1. To see all money that is owed, select Finances in the sidebar.
  2. Select Outstanding. The Aged Debtors page opens.
  3. To see the account of one owner, select Statement.

Common problems

I see "Owner doesn't have an email address". What do I do?
Add an email address to the owner, then select Send Invoice again.
A charge is not on the invoice. Why?
The charge may have a date after the end of the invoice period. Then it goes on the next invoice. Also, check the owner in Bill To (Owner) on the charge.
Why can I not delete an invoice?
You can delete only a draft. To lower what the owner owes on a sent invoice, issue a credit note.
