Your profile has your name, email, phone and photo. It also has your password, two-step sign-in and your notification choices.

Open your profile

  1. On a computer, select your profile card at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Select Your profile.
  3. On a phone, select More, then select your name.

Change your details

  1. Select Details.
  2. Change your first name, last name, email or mobile phone.
  3. Select Save details.

Password reset emails go to your email address. Another user cannot have the same email address.

Add or remove your photo

  1. Select Details.
  2. Select Add a photo or Change photo.
  3. Choose a JPEG, PNG or iPhone photo. The maximum size is 5 MB.
  4. Select Save details.

To remove your photo, select Remove my photo, then select Save details.

Change your password

  1. Select Security.
  2. Type your current password.
  3. Type your new password two times.
  4. Select Change password.

A new password signs you out on your other devices. This device stays signed in.

Turn on two-step sign-in

Two-step sign-in asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone, after your password.

  1. Select Security.
  2. Select Turn on.
  3. Follow the steps on the page that opens.
  4. Keep your recovery codes in a safe place.

Some roles must use two-step sign-in. For these roles, you cannot turn it off.

Sign out

  1. On a computer, select your profile card, then select Sign out.
  2. On a phone, select More, then select Sign Out.

To sign out on all your other devices, open Account security and select Sign Out Other Devices.

Common problems

I forgot my password. What do I do?
On the sign-in page, select Forgot your password?. Cgate sends a link to your email address.
I lost my phone with the authenticator app. What do I do?
Sign in with a recovery code. Then set up two-step sign-in again on your new phone. If you have no recovery codes, ask an administrator to reset two-step sign-in for you.
I sign in with Google. Why can I not change my password?
Your account has no password. To make one, use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page.
