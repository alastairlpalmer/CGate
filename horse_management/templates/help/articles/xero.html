Cgate can send your invoices to Xero as drafts. When an invoice is paid in Xero, Cgate marks it as paid.

Connect Xero

  1. Select Settings in the sidebar.
  2. Select Billing, then Integrations.
  3. Next to Xero, select Connect.
  4. Sign in to Xero.
  5. Select your Xero organisation and allow access.

Xero then sends you back to Settings. The Xero row shows "Connected to" and the name of your organisation.

You need full access to Xero integration and to Business settings to connect Xero.

If you connect a different Xero organisation, Cgate removes the links to the old organisation. Push the invoices again to send them to the new organisation.

Send an invoice to Xero

  1. Select Invoices in the sidebar.
  2. Open the invoice.
  3. Select Push to Xero.
  4. In Xero, check and approve the draft invoice.

To send many invoices, tick them on the Invoices page. Then select Push to Xero in the bar at the bottom of the screen.

After the push, the invoice shows In Xero (Draft). You cannot push a cancelled invoice.

Cgate does not send credit notes to Xero. Enter each credit note in Xero yourself.

Get payment status from Xero

Each night, Cgate asks Xero about each invoice that you pushed. Cgate also asks when you open the invoice page.

  • When Xero shows the invoice as paid, Cgate records a payment and marks the invoice as paid. The invoice shows Paid in Xero. Cgate then sends no more overdue reminders for it.
  • When the invoice is voided in Xero, Cgate cancels the invoice in Cgate.

Xero problems also show in the Money category of your notifications.

Push an invoice again after an error

  1. Read the message that Cgate shows after the push.
  2. Correct the problem, for example in Xero.
  3. Open the invoice again.
  4. Select Retry Xero.

Cgate shows Retry Xero in place of Push to Xero when the last push failed.

Connect Xero again when the connection expires

The connection expires when Cgate does not use it for 60 days, or when Xero stops it. Then you see "Xero session expired. Please reconnect." or the notification "Xero: the connection has expired".

  1. In Settings, select Billing, then Integrations.
  2. Next to Xero, select Connect.
  3. Sign in to Xero and select the same organisation.

Cgate keeps the links to the invoices that you pushed before.

Common problems

Why is there no Push to Xero button?
Xero is not connected, or your role does not have full access to Xero integration.
I see "More than one Xero organisation is connected to Cgate". What do I do?
In Xero, open Settings, Connected apps. Remove Cgate from the organisations that you do not use, then connect again.
I disconnected Xero. Is Cgate still in my Xero account?
Usually no. If Cgate shows a warning, remove Cgate in Xero, in Settings, Connected apps.
