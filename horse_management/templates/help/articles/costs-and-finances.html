The Costs page records what the yard spends, for example hay, rent and staff. The Finances page compares your revenue with your costs.

Add a yard cost

  1. Select Costs in the sidebar.
  2. Select Add Cost, then Yard Cost.
  3. Select the Category.
  4. Enter the Description, the amount and the Date.
  5. If necessary, enter the Supplier, the VAT Amount and a Receipt Image.
  6. Select Add Yard Cost.

A cost that comes again, for example rent, can show as Recurring. Set this in Options on the form. To copy a yard cost with the date of today, select the button Duplicate with today's date next to the cost.

You need full access to Costs to add, change or delete a cost.

Add a cost that the owner pays

  1. On the Costs page, select Add Cost.
  2. Select Rechargeable Cost.
  3. Complete the charge form, then select Add Charge.

A rechargeable cost is an extra charge. It goes on the owner's next invoice. Until then, its status is Unbilled.

Find and filter costs

  1. On the Costs page, select Month, Quarter or Year.
  2. To show fewer costs, open the filters.
  3. Select a Category.
  4. In Type, select Yard costs only or Rechargeable only.

The boxes at the top show This Month, This Year, Rechargeable Unbilled and Yard Costs (Month).

Keep a list of service providers

  1. In Settings, select Yard setup, then Service providers.
  2. Select Add Provider.
  3. Enter the Name and select the Type, for example a farrier or a vet.
  4. Select Add Provider.

You can also select Providers on the Charges page. You need full access to Business settings to add or change a provider.

Read the Finances page

  1. Select Finances in the sidebar.
  • Outstanding: the money that owners owe. Select it to open Aged Debtors.
  • Unbilled Charges: charges that are not on an invoice yet.
  • Revenue This Month: the paid and sent invoices of this month.
  • Costs This Month: the charges and yard costs of this month.

The Revenue vs Costs chart shows each month. A dashed line is a forecast. Site Capacity shows the number of horses and the capacity of each site.

To check your figures against Xero, select Reconciliation. This page shows the totals month by month. To keep a copy, select Download CSV.

Access to the Finances page is all or nothing. A role has full access or no access.

Common problems

Why is Finances not in my sidebar?
Your role does not have access to the Finances overview. Ask an administrator to change your role.
Why can I not delete a rechargeable cost?
The cost is already on an invoice. You can delete only an unbilled charge.
Does a recurring cost add itself each month?
No. To add the cost again, select Duplicate with today's date next to it. Then change the date if necessary.
