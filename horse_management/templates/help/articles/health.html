The Health page keeps the vaccinations, worming treatments, egg counts, conditions and vet visits for all your horses. It also shows which work is due and which work is overdue.

Find your way on the Health page

  1. Select Health in the sidebar.
  2. Select a tab: Overview, Vaccinations, Worming, Egg Counts, Conditions or Vet Visits.
  3. To show the records for one horse, select Filters, then select the horse in Horse.

The Overview tab shows Action Required (overdue vaccinations and vet follow-ups) and Coming Up (work due in the next 30 days). It also shows High Egg Counts and Active Conditions.

Farrier work is not on the Health page. It is on the Farrier page.

Record a vaccination

  1. On the Health page, select the Vaccinations tab.
  2. Select Add Vaccination.
  3. Select the horse and the vaccination type.
  4. Enter the date given.
  5. Select Add Vaccination.

If you do not enter the next due date, Cgate calculates it from the vaccination type. To record one more vaccination after this one, select Save & Add Another.

You need full access to Health to record health work.

Record worming, an egg count or a vet visit

  1. On the Health page, select the Worming, Egg Counts or Vet Visits tab.
  2. Select Add Treatment, Add Egg Count or Add Vet Visit.
  3. Complete the form.
  4. Select the button at the bottom of the form to save the record.

If you enter a cost above 0, Cgate adds a charge to the owner's next invoice. You need full access to Charges to see the cost field.

Record work for several horses at the same time

  1. Select Horses in the sidebar.
  2. Tick the box next to each horse.
  3. In the bar at the bottom of the screen, open Choose action....
  4. Select the work, for example Record Vaccination or Record Worming.
  5. Select Apply to Selected.
  6. Complete the form, then select Record for and the number of horses.

You can also tick horses on an owner's page or on a location's page. A cost on this form is charged in full to the owner of each horse.

Understand "due" and "overdue"

  • Due Soon: the next due date is near. For a vaccination, this period is the reminder days of its vaccination type.
  • Overdue: the next due date is in the past.
  • Replaced: a newer record for the same work replaced this record. A replaced record is never overdue.
  • OK: the work is not due yet.

To show only this work, select Filters on the Vaccinations tab. Then select Due Soon or Overdue in Status.

Common problems

Why is there no cost field on the form?
A cost makes a charge for the owner. Your role needs full access to Charges to see the cost field.
I entered a cost, but no charge was made. Why?
The horse has no current owner. Give the horse an owner, then save the record again.
Why is Record Vaccination not in the list at the bottom of the Horses page?
Your role does not have full access to Health. Ask an administrator to change your role.
An old vaccination does not show as overdue. Why?
A newer vaccination of the same type replaced it. Cgate uses only the newest record to find the next due date.
