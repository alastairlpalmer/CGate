Notifications tell you what needs doing, when horses arrive, move or leave, and when an invoice is paid or becomes overdue.

Open your notifications

  1. On a computer, select Notifications near the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. On a phone, select the bell at the top left of the screen.

The number next to Notifications, or on the bell, is how many notifications you have not read.

Use the tabs and filters

  • New shows the notifications that you have not read.
  • Needs action shows all the work that is overdue or due today, the most urgent first.
  • All shows all your notifications, also the work that is done.

Select a filter, for example Overdue, Health or Money, to show only those notifications.

Do the work that needs doing

  1. Select Needs action.
  2. Find the work, for example a vaccination that is overdue.
  3. Select the button on the notification, for example Record vaccination.
  4. Complete the form and save it.

When the work is done, the notification closes. You do not have to close it.

The dashboard shows how many things need doing. Select that line to open Needs action.

Mark notifications as read

  1. Select the dot on the right of a notification to mark it as read.
  2. To mark all of them as read, select Mark all as read.

When you select the title of a notification, Cgate opens its page and marks it as read. Cgate deletes notifications 90 days after you read them.

Choose your notifications

  1. Select the sliders icon at the top of the notification panel. You can also open Your profile and select Notifications.
  2. For each type, select In the app, By email, or both.
  3. Select Save choices.

Emails about horse movements and money come when the change occurs. Emails about Needs action come as one message each morning.

Dismiss work for the whole yard

An administrator can remove a needs-action notification for all users.

  1. Select Dismiss on the notification.
  2. To show all dismissed work again, select Restore at the bottom of the panel.

Dismiss does not change the record. The work stays on its own page, for example on the Health page.

Common problems

I moved a horse, but I have no notification. Why?
Cgate does not tell you about the changes that you make. Other users get the notification.
I do not get emails. What do I do?
Make sure that your profile has an email address. Then make sure that By email is selected for that type.
Some work does not show in Needs action. Why?
Your role may not give you access to that area. Also, your yard can turn off some reminders in Settings, under Reminders.
A notification stays after I did the work. Why?
Cgate checks the list each hour and when you open the panel. Close the panel and open it again after a few minutes.
