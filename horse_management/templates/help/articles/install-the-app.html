You can put Cgate on your phone or tablet as an app. It opens from an icon, on the full screen. It is the same Cgate, with the same sign-in.

Install on an iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Cgate in Safari and sign in.
  2. Select Share in the browser's bar.
  3. Select Add to Home Screen. If you do not see it, move the list up.
  4. Select Add.
  5. Open Cgate from its icon and sign in one more time. An iPhone keeps the sign-in of the app apart from the sign-in of Safari.

Install on an Android phone

  1. Open Cgate in Chrome and sign in.
  2. On the dashboard, select Install the app.
  3. Select Install.

If the card does not show on the dashboard, open the browser's menu (the three dots) and select Add to Home screen or Install app.

What the app does

  • It opens on the full screen, with no browser bars.
  • It starts faster, because the phone keeps the app's own files.
  • You stay signed in for the same time as in a browser.
  • When a page cannot load, you see No signal and Try again. The page loads by itself when the signal is back.

You need a signal to see your records and to save a record. The phone does not keep your horses, your owners or your invoices: it keeps only the app's own files.

Remove the app

Remove it as you remove other apps on your phone. Your records are not on the phone, so nothing is lost.

Common problems

I do not see Add to Home Screen on my iPhone. Why?
Use Safari. If you opened Cgate from a link in another app, open it in Safari first. Then select Share and move the list up.
The card Use Cgate as an app does not show on the dashboard. Why?
The card shows only on a phone or a tablet, and only in a browser. It does not show after you closed it, and it does not show in the app. Use the steps on this page.
The app asks me to sign in again. Is this correct?
Yes, one time. An iPhone keeps the sign-in of the app apart from the sign-in of Safari.
I see No signal. Did I lose my record?
No. That page shows when a page cannot load. A record that you were typing in a form stays in the form: the form tells you when a save did not go.
