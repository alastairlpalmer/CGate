The Feed area shows how much feed you have at each site. Use it to log deliveries, record feed that you give out to a location, and correct the stock.

See your feed stock

  1. Open Feed in the sidebar. The Feed Store page opens.
  2. Read the cards at the top. Each card shows the stock of one feed type at one site.
  3. To show less, select a site in All sites or a type in All feed types.
  4. Read the Stock Ledger to see each delivery, feed out and adjustment.

On a phone, select More at the bottom of the screen, then select Feed.

Log a feed delivery

  1. On the Feed Store page, select Log Delivery.
  2. Select the Site where you keep the feed.
  3. Select the Feed Type and the Date.
  4. Type the Quantity and select the Unit, for example Round Bales.
  5. If you know it, type the Supplier and the Total Cost.
  6. Select Log Delivery.

When you type a Total Cost, Cgate also makes a yard cost record for the delivery.

Record feed that you give out

  1. Open Locations and open the location that gets the feed.
  2. Select Feed Out.
  3. Select the Feed Type and the Date.
  4. Type the Quantity and select the Unit.
  5. Leave Total Cost empty to calculate it from the cost of your last delivery.
  6. To bill the owners, select Recharge this cost to horse owners in this location.
  7. Select the horses that share the cost, or select Select All.
  8. Select Record Feed Out.

Cgate divides the cost equally between the horses that you select. It adds a charge for each horse to its owner. You need full access to Feed to record a feed out.

Correct the stock

  1. On the Feed Store page, select Adjust Stock.
  2. Select the Site, the Feed Type and the Unit.
  3. Type the Quantity as a positive number.
  4. In Reason, select Adjustment to add stock, or Waste to remove stock.
  5. In Notes, write why you make the change.
  6. Select Apply Adjustment.

Find feed that is low

  1. Open your notifications and select Needs action.
  2. Find the feed item. It shows the feed type, the site, and how much is left.
  3. Select the item. The Feed Store page opens and shows only that feed.
  4. When the new feed arrives, select Log Delivery.

Cgate shows feed as low when less than 10 units are left. On the Feed Store page, a low card shows its number in a different colour.

Common problems

I recorded a feed out, but the stock did not go down. Why?
The feed out must have a Quantity and a Unit. The site, feed type and unit must be the same as on the delivery.
The total cost of a feed out is £0.00. Why?
Cgate found no delivery with a cost for that site, feed type and unit. Type the Total Cost yourself.
A horse did not get a feed charge. Why?
The horse has no current owner. Cgate does not charge a horse that has no owner.
I do not see low stock in my notifications. Why?
The Feed stock reminder can be off. An administrator can turn it on in Settings, in Reminders.
