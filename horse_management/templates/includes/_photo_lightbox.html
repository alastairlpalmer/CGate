{% comment %} Full-screen photo viewer — rendered once in base.html. Any element with `data-lightbox-src` opens it (see static/js/lightbox.js), so a horse photo can be seen large from the list without leaving the page. Triggers that share a `data-lightbox-group` make a gallery: swipe sideways, press the arrow keys, or use the arrow buttons to step through it. The overlay is `touch-none`, which keeps a sideways swipe for the gallery — without it the browser reads one as its own back gesture and leaves the page. It sits above the pop-up sheet (z-70 > z-60). Escape, the scrim, the close button and a tap on the photo all close it. {% endcomment %}
