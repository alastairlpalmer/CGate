The problem

A yard manager keeps track of many horses, many owners and many dates. Who owns which horse. Which stable it is in. When the farrier is due. What each owner must pay this month.

Most of this lives on whiteboards, in notebooks and in spreadsheets. Invoices take days. Health dates get missed. Money is lost when a stay or an extra is not billed.

What we believe

  • Record it once.A stay, a visit or a charge goes in once. The invoices, the reminders and the records follow from it.
  • Simple on a phone.The work happens in the yard, not at a desk. Cgate is made for one hand and a busy day.
  • Everyone connected.Yard, owners, vets and farriers see the same facts about each horse.
  • Your data is yours.Safe, backed up every day, and yours to take with you.
