Prefers the generated square thumbnail (~10-20 KB) over the full-resolution original (often several MB); falls back to the original when no thumb exists (e.g. photos uploaded before thumbnails, until generate_thumbnails is run). A photo also opens the full-screen viewer on click or Enter (see static/js/lightbox.js), so the horse can be seen large from any list without leaving the page. The viewer shows the full-resolution original and, from the small sizes, a link to the horse page — the hero (lg) is already on it. The small sizes share the "horses" gallery group, so a swipe (or an arrow key or button) in the viewer goes to the next horse on the page that has a photo. The hero is one photo on its own — the Photos card below it holds that horse's gallery. The small sizes are `relative z-10`, which lifts the photo above the stretched .list-card-link::after overlay. Without that, a tap on the photo opens the horse page instead of the viewer.
